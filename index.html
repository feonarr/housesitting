About me

Hi, I'm Feona.

Hey, I'm Fe! I'm a remote worker in a fun in-between chapter — no lease, lots of flexibility, and genuinely excited to be somewhere that feels like home while you're off having adventures.

I'm the person who will actually water your plants on schedule, send you unprompted updates about your cat, and leave your place cleaner than I found it. I love animals, cozy spaces, and taking good care of things — it's just how I'm wired.

If you're heading out and want someone you trust keeping the lights on, I'd love to be that person. Let's make it easy.
